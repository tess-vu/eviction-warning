Model Context

Features Used:

  • Monthly eviction filing counts (Eviction Lab)
  • Tract-level demographics: renter share, poverty rate, severe rent burden, single-mother households, median income (ACS)
  • Spatial lag of neighboring tract filings
  • Real estate tax delinquency counts (OPA / Revenue)
  • Moratorium status indicator
  • 3-month moving average of filings

What the model does not account for:

  • Sealed or expunged filing records
  • Same-month economic shocks (job losses, plant closures)
  • Informal evictions and lease non-renewals
  • Landlord-specific behavior or portfolio changes

Validation: Trained on 2020–2023 monthly panel. Tested on 2024 hold-out. Model MAE on Test Set: {{ model_mae }}.
